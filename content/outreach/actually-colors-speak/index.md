## Actually, Colors Speak

**In collaboration with Ipsa Jain (@ipsawonders)**

> *"There are a lot of popular science books that discuss astronomy and natural history, but there are seldom that bridge cell and molecular biology to a particular natural phenomenon. As scientists from a reputed science institution in India, we aspire to fill this niche through an illustrated popular science book."*

### About the Book

In this book, we chose to explain **colors in animals**—because animals come in a stunning variety of colors and many can rapidly change colors in response to environmental cues.

While researching, we found that changes in exterior skin color originate deep inside the cells. The fascinating aspect is that animals capable of changing color within minutes rely on a coordinated network of intracellular proteins called the **cytoskeleton**. 

Our lab at iBRIC-inStem, Bangalore investigates cytoskeleton proteins at a fundamental level. To showcase the transitions from skin tissue to individual cells and intracellular molecular elements, we adapted a graphic illustration style. To complement these illustrations, we narrated the science behind cellular and molecular changes as an engaging conversation between three characters.

We hope our book inspires new generations of students to engage in scientific curiosities and ask fundamental questions when exploring nature.
