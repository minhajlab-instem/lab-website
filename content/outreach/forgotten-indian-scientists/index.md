## Written Out of History: Forgotten Indian Scientists

*In Collaboration with Munmun Dhalaria (Moon Peak Films), funded by the DBT/Wellcome Trust India Alliance Public Engagement Award and DBT-inStem, Bangalore.*

This documentary film revives the memory and achievements of three unsung heroes from three decades of Indian science who made seminal contributions to medicine, botany, and biology:

### The Scientists

1. **Dr. Sambhunath De (1915–1985)**  
   The medical scientist who discovered the **Cholera enterotoxin** in 1959. His pioneering discovery transformed our physiological understanding of cholera and laid the foundation for modern oral rehydration therapy and vaccine development. Why did a scientist who helped tame an epidemic disease get confined only to a footnote in medical textbooks?

2. **Dr. Sipra Guha-Mukherjee (1938–2007)**  
   An Indian botanist who co-discovered the technique of **haploid plant generation through anther culture** in 1964 with Satish Chandra Maheshwari. What struggles did she face as a pioneering woman researcher in a male-dominated scientific ecosystem?

3. **Dr. Obaid Siddiqi (1932–2013)**  
   An exemplary geneticist, neurobiologist, and the visionary founder-director of the **National Centre for Biological Sciences (NCBS, TIFR)** in Bangalore, whose intellect, ethos, and mentorship shaped modern biological research in India.
