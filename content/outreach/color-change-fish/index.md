## Color Change in Fish — Biological Explanation & DIY Instructions

Fish and amphibians exhibit dynamic color change driven by specialized pigment-containing cells called **chromatophores** (such as black/brown melanophores).

Inside these cells, pigment granules (melanosomes) are transported along microtubule tracks powered by kinesin and dynein molecular motors. When melanosomes disperse outward to the cell periphery, the tissue appears dark; when they aggregate tightly around the cell nucleus, the tissue appears pale or translucent.
