## Overview

Heart muscle contraction depends on the exquisite structural alignment of sarcomeres, microtubules, and intermediate filaments within cardiomyocytes. Disruptions in cytoskeletal integrity or myofibrillar orientation lead to severe hypertrophic and dilated cardiomyopathies.

## Our Approach

By integrating **optical tissue clearing, 3D micrometer-scale imaging, patient-derived iPSCs, and CRISPR-edited cardiomyocyte models**, our group investigates:
1. Long-axis fiber continuums in ventricular heart walls and how cardiomyocyte orientation is perturbed in disease.
2. The mechanistic role of tubulin tyrosine ligase (TTL) mutations and perturbed tubulin tyrosination in promoting cardiomyocyte hypertrophy.
3. Ultrastructural organization of cardiomyocytes and nuclear envelope mechanotransduction.
