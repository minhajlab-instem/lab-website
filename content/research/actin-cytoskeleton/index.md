## Overview

The actin cytoskeleton is the primary structural scaffold driving cell shape, mechanical rigidity, cell motility, and cytokinesis. In addition to canonical cytoplasmic actin networks, non-canonical **nuclear actin** assemblies are increasingly recognized as vital regulators of chromatin organization, transcription, and DNA repair.

## Our Approach

Fluorescent probes such as LifeAct, UtrCH, and Phalloidin are widely used in cell biology to visualize actin structures, yet their molecular interactions and structural fidelity had long remained poorly defined at atomic resolution.

Our lab utilizes **high-resolution cryo-EM** to determine how actin filament markers interact with F-actin, revealing structural determinants of binding and guiding the rational design of minimally disruptive probes. Concurrently, we are investigating the biochemical regulation and ultrastructure of nuclear actin assemblies in mammalian cells.
