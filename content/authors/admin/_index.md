### About Minhaj Sirajuddin

Minhaj Sirajuddin is a Professor / Scientist-G at the Institute for Stem Cell Science and Regenerative Medicine (iBRIC-inStem), Bangalore, India. 

His research focuses on understanding eukaryotic biological motions across orders of magnitude scale involving cytoskeleton elements. Mutations in cytoskeleton components are frequently associated with human pathology, including cardiomyopathies, neurological disorders, and ciliopathies. The cytoskeleton lab investigates these systems using cryo-EM, biochemistry, cell biology, and advanced microscopy to bridge the knowledge gap between clinical findings and molecular mechanisms.

**Scientific Interests:** Biology across scales — from single molecules to organ level function.

**Outside the Lab:** Running, biking, and photography.
