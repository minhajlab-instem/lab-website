        We are always looking for passionate and driven researchers with backgrounds in **biochemistry, structural biology, biophysics, cell biology, or computational biology**.

        ### 🎓 Graduate Students (PhD)
        Students interested in PhD research in our lab should apply through the **TIFR-JGEEBILS** nationwide entrance examination, followed by institutional interviews at inStem/NCBS.

        ### 🔬 Postdoctoral Researchers
        Prospective postdocs with a strong track record in structural biology (cryo-EM/X-ray), reconstitution biochemistry, or advanced optical microscopy are encouraged to write directly to Prof. Minhaj Sirajuddin with a CV, a brief research statement, and contact details for three references. We actively support competitive fellowship applications (e.g., DBT/Wellcome Trust India Alliance, EMBO, HFSP, SERB-NPDF).
