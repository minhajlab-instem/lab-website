        Our lab shares standardized biochemistry and biophysics protocols for cytoskeleton reconstitution, molecular motor purifications, and in vitro motility assays.

        ### Tubulin & Microtubules
        - **Tubulin Preparation from Brain Tissue**: Purification from porcine or goat brain via temperature-dependent polymerization/depolymerization cycles.
        - [**Recombinant Yeast Tubulin Preparation**](https://drive.google.com/open?id=0B_Z7cPRqQbh-R1BPdE8yd3Z3T28): Expression and purification of tagged tubulin heterodimers from *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*.
        - [**Microtubule Polymerization & Polarity Marking**](https://drive.google.com/open?id=0B_Z7cPRqQbh-V1hTLUZPZ3VXYzQ): GMPCPP and Taxol-stabilized seeds with fluorescent polarity marking for TIRF assays.
        - **Kinesin-1 (K560-GFP) Expression & Purification**: Recombinant expression in *E. coli* and affinity chromatography.
        - [**Microtubule Binding & Release (MTBR)**](https://drive.google.com/open?id=0B_Z7cPRqQbh-MDhEVHdZdnNhanc): Enrichment of functional active motor proteins via nucleotide-dependent binding and ATP release.
        - [**Pf-MCAK (Kinesin-13) Purification**](https://drive.google.com/open?id=0B_Z7cPRqQbh-UGhVN0NsUUxmVGs): Expression, purification, and depolymerase activity assays.

        ---

        ### Actomyosin System
        - **Actin Preparation from Chicken Skeletal Muscle**: Acetone powder preparation, G-actin extraction, and ultracentrifugation purification.
        - **Muscle Myosin II Preparation**: Purification from chicken skeletal and bovine heart muscle tissue.
        - **Deadheading Enrichment**: Centrifugal separation to enrich fully active, non-rigor myosin motors.
        - **In Vitro Motility Assays**: Gliding filament assays on nitrocellulose-coated flow cells using fluorescent actin.

        ---

        ### General Lab Reagents & Molecular Biology
        - **Gibson Assembly Master Mixture**: Isothermal DNA assembly reaction cocktail preparation.
        - **Chemically Competent Cells**: High-efficiency rubidium chloride / calcium chloride preparation protocol.
        - **Trolox / Enzymatic Oxygen Scavenging Mixture**: Anti-photobleaching system for high-resolution single-molecule fluorescence microscopy.
